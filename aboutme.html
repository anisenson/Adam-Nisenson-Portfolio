About Me


Hi, I'm Adam Nisenson

I am a current senior at Marlboro High School, graduating this year and set to begin my studies at Penn State University this fall, with my intended major in Cybersecurity. Over the past three years, I have been part of the school’s Web Design CTE Pathway Program, which sparked my passion for technology. Throughout my time in this program, I’ve created websites both in and outside of class, turning it into a side hustle and gaining real-world experience in web development.

My interest in cybersecurity deepened during my junior year when I took a class and participated in CyberStart America, a nationwide cybersecurity competition. I earned recognition as a National Cyber Scholar by the National Cyber Scholarship Foundation. This year, I expanded on that experience through a hands-on internship with the NJCCIC, where I helped develop and test Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges for JerseyCTF.

Outside of tech, I work part-time as a busser and banquet server. I love playing golf with friends and family, and I’m a big New York sports fan- especially the Yankees and Jets. In my free time, I enjoy playing poker or casual pickup basketball.
